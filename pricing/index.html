Pricing FAQs

Questions we get asked.

Is there a minimum commitment?
No 12-month contract. The Dedicated Resource model has a one-calendar-month notice period either way. Ad Hoc has no minimum.
Do you charge for onboarding?
No. We charge for productive work delivered. Time spent learning your processes, templates and software preferences is on us.
Are there any costs beyond the rate?
No. No management fees, software charges, infrastructure fees or platform markups. What you see on this page is what you pay.
How do you handle GDPR and data security?
Every engagement begins with a signed GDPR-aligned Data Processing Agreement. We work inside your software environment wherever possible. Where we hold data, access is role-based, transfer is encrypted, and handling is GDPR-compliant.
Can I switch models?
Yes. Many clients start on Ad Hoc and move to a Dedicated Resource once they have seen the quality of the work. No penalty, no new minimum term.
What software do you work in?
QuickBooks, Sage, FreeAgent, IRIS, BrightPay, TaxCalc, Capium, Dext, Hubdoc, Moneysoft. Xero: our team is fluent and Xero certification is currently in progress.
Are your team members UK-qualified?
Our team holds Indian Chartered Accountant (ICAI) qualifications, not ACCA or ICAEW. Indian CAs are trained in IFRS and work fluently in UK GAAP, MTD and HMRC processes. If your firm's compliance framework specifically requires ACCA or ICAEW membership on outsourced files, we recommend confirming that requirement before engaging us.
